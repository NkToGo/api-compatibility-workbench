rubric/contract and all selected operational adapter/runtime records. It includes
the failed workers, uncited original claims, coordinator decisions, follow-up
history, reconciliation and every retained public model-response text field.

remains in its original trace. No protected expectation is inserted into an agent's

This review uses unblinded model-assisted offline inspection, with no independent
human adjudication, calibrated reviewer-accuracy measurement or new provider API
call. The reviewer inspected the 37 distinct public prose passages and decomposed
their 98 recorded occurrences into 264 proposition judgments. Exact repeated text
can reuse its inspected clause judgment; final endorsement is assessed separately
from direct citation labels. A claim being available or cited does not make it
endorsed or correct.

The deterministic answer's 63 normalized diagnostic records were independently
recomputed from the original captured observations and order records without

review enumerates 1,161 scalar/empty-collection diagnostic assertions. Availability,
admission, execution/transport status, actual-fact presence, identity/version

recognized diagnostic record structure; it is not the review's semantic verdict.

The complete catalog, fixed mandatory prefix, alias-hash stratum ordering,

There are 1,442 judgment records in total: the diagnostic assertions, prose clauses

Hashes and pointers verify local byte consistency. They do not authenticate the

## Material issue and retained failure history

The single-agent final limitation states that comparison adapters were unavailable.
Its cited metadata sets `comparisonAdaptersAvailable: false` for the earlier
capture/disclosure description. The later single-agent adapter nevertheless
completed this investigation and retained 22 model responses. Applying that
disclosure-stage flag to the later investigation is unsupported. The metadata's
literal flag is preserved; its scope is the problem.

That same assertion occurs in final finding `unit-11` and raw finish response
`unit-23`, with endorsement retained in both. The review therefore records **two
unsupported material occurrences of one underlying scope error**, not two
independent failures. It records no material unresolved occurrence under the
declared judgments. Absence of a recorded unresolved judgment is not proof that
the reviewer found every possible error.

The team history also needs a precise scope distinction. No accepted substantive
webhook report was supplied to final reconciliation. Original webhook tool reads
and the rejected fresh response still exist. The final statement about missing
findings/evidence is interpreted as the absent accepted report; it must not be
used to claim that no historical reads or rejected prose existed.

The fresh worker consumed 26 model opportunities: 25 catalog decisions followed by
its rejected finish response. Its 26 tool reads were one description plus 25
catalog pages, with no `read_text` page. Its last turn had one remaining decision.
The claim of no freshly read citable text is supported as an access limitation at
that final turn. It does not mean readable evidence was absent from the bundle or
that the earlier catalog-only choices were unavoidable. The full team's original
55 model reservations and 53 tool reads remain in the operational record.
