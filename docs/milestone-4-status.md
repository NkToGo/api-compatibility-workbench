# Milestone 4 status

The task isolation, parallel scheduling, coordinator/follow-up/reconciliation
boundaries and four comparison adapters are implemented on one trusted host.
The frozen known-case campaign completed its twelve planned trials. Separate
review and the paired report preserve the declared denominator, original claims,
unsupported/unresolved findings and inclusive resource/timing records.
The [scoped routing policy](coordination-routing.md) is implemented as a read-only
diagnostics-first recommendation with mandatory maintainer review.

**Milestone 4 acceptance is not established.** Its declared criterion remains
useful coordination on heterogeneous consumers under declared budgets, without
permission leakage or unbounded delegation. Successful orchestration and boundary
tests do not establish the usefulness part of that criterion.

The [recorded comparison](coordination-results.md) supplied no accepted whole
bundle and no adaptive accepted case under its model-assisted known-case review.
The team added aggregate resource use without improving accepted-case counts.
The recorded rubric judges separate case answers, not a joint cross-consumer
decision. These measurements cannot support
an automatic team default or a general conclusion that coordination never helps.
Incomplete reasoning and one unresolved claim remain explicit acceptance gaps.

## Acceptance follow-up

Before closing the milestone or promoting automatic team routing:

1. Define a separately identified migration workload with an actual shared
   consumer assumption and declared joint acceptance criteria. Select it for a
   real maintainer need; do not construct the grading criteria to favor a team.
2. Establish independently held expected behavior and complete diagnostics, then
   freeze case rights/provenance, source/configuration/evidence identities, review
   method, final selection, simpler baselines, budgets and ordered trial slots.
   Any new model, prompt or implementation is a new condition, not a repair of
   the existing campaign history.
3. Evaluate fresh outcomes under those declared conditions, reviewing every
   material claim and retaining operational failures, negative results,
   uncertainty, review effort and inclusive resource/time accounting.
4. Demonstrate useful coordination under the original budget/permission criterion
   before claiming acceptance. If it remains unestablished, retain simpler routing
   and the explicit gap; do not select favorable retries or lower the rubric.

The existing campaign, rubric, review and reports remain historical evidence and
must not be rerun or rewritten simply to obtain a preferred result. Broader provider
support, live repository inventory, publication authorization and external product
use still require their separately planned implementation and validation.
